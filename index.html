About Me

Civil Engineering Background.
AI Engineering Direction.

Progressing from YOLO-based pavement damage detection to 2D GPR research, multi-view deep learning and containerized AI deployment.

Building AI systems, not only models.

I started working with Computer Vision during my B.Eng. capstone project by developing YOLO-based pavement damage detection models and presenting the work at KMUTT Project Day 2024.

I later continued AI research at the M.Eng. level, focusing on Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) cavity detection and progressing from 2D single-view object detection to multi-view deep learning with TriView-YOLO.

My current focus is end-to-end AI development: training and evaluating models, building usable AI applications, containerizing applications using Docker and Docker Compose, and publishing portable AI images through Docker Hub.
