About Me

Civil Engineering Background.
AI Engineering Direction.

My work progressed from YOLO-based pavement damage detection to 2D GPR cavity detection, multi-view deep learning and deployable AI applications.

Building AI systems, not only models.

I started working with Computer Vision during my B.Eng. capstone project, developing YOLO-based pavement damage detection models and presenting the project at KMUTT Project Day 2024.

I later continued AI research at the M.Eng. level, focusing on Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) cavity detection — progressing from 2D single-view object detection to multi-view deep learning with TriView-YOLO.

My current focus is end-to-end AI development: training and evaluating models, building usable web interfaces, containerizing AI applications and making models portable through reproducible deployment workflows.
