Pavement Damage Detection with YOLO

Undergraduate computer-vision project applying real-time object detection to pavement damage assessment.

Benchmarked YOLOv8, YOLOv9 and YOLOv9-GELAN variants for pavement damage detection.
Classified 10 pavement-damage categories using road images from multiple countries including Thailand.
Evaluated 21 model/setting combinations across Normal, Background and Augmentation conditions.
Presented at KMUTT Project Day 2024.
10 Damage Classes
21 Model Settings
0.598 Best mAP
